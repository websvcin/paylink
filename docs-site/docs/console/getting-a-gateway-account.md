# Getting a gateway account

If the platform runs a partner programme for a gateway, its card on **Payment methods** has **Get an account** and the platform's headline (e.g. "Get PayU in 2 minutes").

- **A partner link** (↗): PayLink records your interest and opens the partner's signup in a new tab, with your account named so the partner knows who you are.
- **A form**: contact name, email and phone, and optionally your registered business name, website or app, expected monthly volume and a note. **Send request**; the platform's team contacts you.

The card then shows **Account: New / In progress / Account ready / Declined**, with any note from the platform team. Approval, pricing and timelines are the gateway's.

When your account is ready, press **I have an account**, enter the credentials and **Check and turn on**.

Scripts: `GET/POST /api/tenant/account-requests`.
